# Product

IT GURUs Germany (https://www.itgurusgermany.com) is a community website for Nigerians and
Africans living in, or moving to, Germany.

- **Guides:** a verified knowledge base (study, visa, work, IT careers, family reunion, housing, health, everyday life)
- **News & events**, a **community/volunteer** page and a **contact** form
- **Editorial workflow:** members write guides; editors review and publish; admins manage roles

Tone: friendly, practical, trustworthy. Guides must point to official sources and never replace legal advice.

## Contribution model
- Clone over SSH, branch off `main`, open a PR. CI must pass before merge.
